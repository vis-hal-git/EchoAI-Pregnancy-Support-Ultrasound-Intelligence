Core Capabilities

Everything your clinical workflow needs

Six specialised AI modules built on ISUOG protocols and evidence-grounded retrieval.

🤰

Pregnancy Triage

Instant three-class classification — Gravid, Non-Gravid, or Pregnancy of Unknown Location (PUL) — from a single scan upload.

🫀

5-Plane Cardiac Screening

Full ISUOG fetal cardiac protocol: 4-chamber, LVOT, RVOT, 3-vessel, and 3VT planes audited for image quality and anatomy.

🧠

Neurosonogram Verification

Three-axial brain plane evaluation — transthalamic, transcerebellar, transventricular — with automatic landmark detection.

📋

Biometric Reports

CRL, BPD, HC, AC, and FL measurements cross-referenced against INTERGROWTH-21 and WHO growth charts with percentile output.

🩺

Structured Clinical Assessment

Instant standardized reports with diagnostic triage, anatomical checklist, biometrics table, and ISUOG guideline citations.
