Dataset Structure

Six Specialised JSONL Datasets

Each dataset addresses a distinct clinical domain. Together they cover the full scope of obstetric ultrasound.

📐
views.jsonl

Standard Planes & Landmarks

Descriptions of every standard scanning plane — transthalamic, LVOT, 4-chamber, abdominal circumference — with anatomical landmark requirements and image quality criteria.

248 records
🔍
findings.jsonl

Sonographic Features & Criteria

Catalogue of ultrasound findings: what each feature looks like, how it is measured, and its diagnostic significance. Includes echogenicity patterns, morphological descriptors, and Doppler waveform profiles.

412 records
🔄
workflows.jsonl

Clinical Protocols & Triage

Step-by-step clinical decision pathways for PUL management, failed pregnancy diagnosis, ectopic exclusion, and referral escalation — drawn from RCOG and SMFM guidelines.

156 records
🫀
diseases.jsonl

Congenital Anomalies & Pathologies

Structured entries for each congenital condition WombCare can flag: Tetralogy of Fallot, Turner syndrome, anencephaly, ventriculomegaly, and 50+ others, with ultrasound appearance and prevalence data.

318 records
📁
cases.jsonl

Verified Clinical Cases

De-identified case summaries with ultrasound findings, clinical context, outcome, and expert commentary. Used to ground the AI in realistic presentation patterns rather than textbook scenarios alone.

289 records
🖼️
media.jsonl

Figure Evidence & Annotations

Descriptions of published ultrasound figures with expert annotations: measurement technique, image quality assessment, and diagnostic interpretation — bridging textual and visual clinical knowledge.

97 records
Retrieval Architecture

Hybrid Three-Backend Retrieval

Every query fans out across three independent retrieval systems. Results are merged and ranked before injection into the system prompt.

Query → Parallel Retrieval → Merge → Top-K Chunks → GPT-4o

🔎 Clinical Query

Extracted from scan context

→

🗄️ SQLite FTS5

Keyword & phrase match · BM25

🧮 ChromaDB

Semantic similarity · cosine

☁️ OpenAI Vector Store

Cloud retrieval · file-search

→

🔀 Merge & Rank

Reciprocal Rank Fusion → Top-K

→

🤖 GPT-4o

Grounded report generation

Why three backends? FTS5 returns exact keyword matches instantly — critical for specific biometric thresholds. ChromaDB catches semantically similar concepts even when terminology differs. The OpenAI Vector Store adds cloud-scale retrieval across the full 68-paper corpus. Fusion prevents any single backend's blind spots from reaching the model.
