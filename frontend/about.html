About WombCare

An internship-born AI system that demonstrates how multimodal large language models can be safely grounded in clinical evidence for obstetric decision support.

The Project

What WombCare is and why it matters

The clinical problem

Fetal ultrasound is the cornerstone of obstetric care — yet access to experienced sonographers is uneven, scan interpretation is variable, and follow-up communication between providers and patients often lacks the specificity that modern clinical practice demands.

In low-resource settings, a single scan may be read by a general practitioner rather than a specialist. Even in well-resourced hospitals, junior staff performing out-of-hours scans benefit enormously from structured decision support that surfaces the right clinical guideline at the right moment.

WombCare's approach

WombCare does not attempt to replace the sonographer. Instead, it acts as a second reader — an AI assistant that has digested 1,520 clinical records, 68 peer-reviewed papers, and the full ISUOG cardiac and neurosonogram screening protocols, and can apply them consistently to any uploaded scan within seconds.

Every statement in a WombCare report is tied to a specific record in the knowledge base. The medico-legal guardrails built into the Master Fusion Prompt ensure the system never oversteps its role as a decision-support tool.

Medical Disclaimer

WombCare is a clinical decision-support tool developed for educational and research purposes as part of an AI engineering internship. It is not a licensed medical device, and its outputs must not be used as the sole basis for any clinical decision. All AI-generated reports must be reviewed, verified, and authorised by a qualified sonographer, obstetrician, or other appropriately credentialled healthcare professional before any clinical action is taken. WombCare does not store patient data, does not provide a definitive diagnosis, and expressly prohibits use as a replacement for professional medical judgement. The developer and associated institution accept no liability for clinical outcomes arising from misuse of this system.
