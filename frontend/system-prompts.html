3-Tier Clinical System Prompt Architecture

Three specialised system prompts orchestrate GPT-4o's behaviour at each stage of ultrasound analysis — from image inspection to evidence grounding to final report generation.

Prompt Design

The Three Prompt Tiers

Click any tier to expand its full specification.

1

Tier 1 — Vision System Prompt

Image Quality Auditing & Standard Plane Recognition

Acoustic Quality Auditing

Scores each frame 0–10 for penetration depth, gain calibration, focal zone placement, and artefact presence. Rejects sub-threshold images before downstream processing.

ISUOG 5-Plane Cardiac Protocol

Identifies 4-chamber, LVOT, RVOT, 3-vessel, and 3VT planes. Flags missing planes and assesses cardiac axis (45° ± 20°) and situs concordance.

3-Axial Neurosonogram Evaluation

Validates transthalamic, transcerebellar, and transventricular planes. Checks cavum septum pellucidum, thalami, vermis, and cisterna magna visualisation.

Biometric Caliper Integrity

Confirms that measurement calipers are placed at anatomically correct landmarks — outer-to-outer for BPD, skin surface for HC, and mid-diaphragm for AC.

2

Tier 2 — RAG Grounding Prompt

Evidence Injection & Clinical Threshold Validation

Evidence Grounding from RAG Knowledge Base

Injects the top-K retrieved chunks from the 1,520-record rag-only database. Every retrieved record is tagged with a [Record X] citation that must appear in the final report.

Validated Clinical Thresholds

Hardcodes ISUOG / SMFM thresholds: MSD ≥ 25 mm (viable IUP), CRL ≥ 7 mm (cardiac activity expected), NT < 3.5 mm (normal), lateral ventricle < 10 mm (normal). Any deviation is flagged.

Risk Stratification

Classifies each finding as Low, Intermediate, or High risk and mandates that the AI justify each classification with a cited clinical record.

Contradiction Detection

Instructs the model to flag when visual findings contradict retrieved guidelines — preventing hallucinations by surfacing uncertainty rather than fabricating resolution.

3

Tier 3 — Master Fusion Prompt

Report Assembly & Medico-Legal Guardrails

3-Class Triage Classification

Forces the model to output exactly one of: GRAVID (confirmed intrauterine pregnancy), NON-GRAVID (no intrauterine pregnancy identified), or PUL (Pregnancy of Unknown Location — IUP not confirmed nor excluded).

Standardised 8-Part Report

Enforces a fixed output schema: (1) Triage Class, (2) Scan Quality, (3) Gestational Age, (4) Biometrics, (5) Anatomical Survey, (6) Cardiac & Neural Screening, (7) Risk Summary, (8) Clinical Recommendation with citations.

Medico-Legal Guardrails

Prohibits definitive diagnosis, mandates specialist referral language, and appends a standard disclaimer: "This report is generated by an AI clinical decision-support tool and must be reviewed by a qualified sonographer or obstetrician."

Multi-Turn Conversation Context

Maintains the full report in context for follow-up questions, allowing clinicians to ask targeted queries without re-uploading the image.
